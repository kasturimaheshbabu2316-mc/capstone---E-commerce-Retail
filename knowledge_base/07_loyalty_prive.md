# Nykaa Privé Loyalty Rewards & Redemption Policy

Nykaa Privé loyalty tier membership allows customers to earn loyalty points on every completed eligible transaction across beauty and fashion. Every 100 reward points equal ten rupees and can be redeemed directly at checkout against product subtotals exceeding five hundred rupees. Privé Gold and Platinum members enjoy complimentary birthday gifts, free shipping vouchers, and early access to pink sale events. Reward points remain valid for 365 calendar days from issuance and cannot be redeemed for cash or transferred between accounts.
