For orders paid via Cash on Delivery (COD), refunds are processed directly into the customer's verified bank account or Nykaa Wallet within 3 to 5 business days after product quality inspection. Customers must input their IFSC code and bank account number through the Nykaa order returns portal to initiate the transfer. In-store store credits or Nykaa wallet balances are credited instantly within 24 hours of return approval. Cash refunds at the doorstep are strictly not provided by logistics delivery partners.
