# Reverse Pickup Eligibility & Logistics Guidelines

Reverse pickup is arranged free of charge across more than 19,000 serviceable postal pin codes in India. Once a return request is authorized, a logistics courier agent will attempt doorstep collection within 24 to 48 business hours. If an address falls in a non-serviceable pin code, the customer must self-ship the parcel via a speed courier, eligible for a maximum reimbursement of one hundred rupees. The pickup agent will conduct an on-spot verification of original packaging, barcode tags, and product seals prior to acceptance.
