# Order Cancellation Policy & Procedure

Orders can be cancelled directly through the Nykaa mobile application or website while they remain in the Placed or Processing status prior to dispatch. Once an order transitions to Shipped or Out for Delivery status, automated cancellation is locked and the customer must refuse package delivery at the doorstep. Prepaid cancellations initiate an immediate automated refund to the original payment source within 3 to 7 working days. Partial item cancellations are permitted if items belong to separate merchant fulfillment nodes.
