# Damaged, Leaked or Tampered Package Claims Procedure

If a parcel is delivered in a damaged, leaked, or visibly tampered condition, customers must raise a claim within 48 hours of delivery. Customers must provide clear photographic and video evidence showing the outer shipping box, courier shipping label, and internal damaged merchandise. Do not discard the packaging materials or product containers until the grievance investigation is finalized by the fraud and logistics team. Verified damage claims result in an expedited priority replacement or 100% full refund within 3 business days.
