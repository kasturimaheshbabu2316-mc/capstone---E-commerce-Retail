Personal styling electronics including hair straighteners, curlers, and facial cleansers carry a standard 12-month manufacturer warranty from the date of purchase. Warranty claims require presenting the original Nykaa digital tax invoice and authentic manufacturer warranty card. Nykaa customer support facilitates direct service-center routing or replacement approvals for verified manufacturing defects. Consumables, cosmetics, skincare liquids, and fragrance sprays do not carry warranty protection once delivered.
