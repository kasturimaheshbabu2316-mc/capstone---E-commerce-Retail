# Payment Failure, Auto-Reconciliation & Retry Protocol

If money is debited from a user's bank or card account during a failed or timed-out transaction, the payment gateway auto-reconciles the amount within 24 to 48 hours. Unassigned bank deductions are automatically reversed back to the source account within 5 to 7 business banking days depending on the acquiring bank. Customers can safely retry the order using UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, or Cash on Delivery after 15 minutes without duplicate billing risk. If the order status remains unconfirmed after 2 hours, customers should submit their bank transaction reference number (RRN) to support.
