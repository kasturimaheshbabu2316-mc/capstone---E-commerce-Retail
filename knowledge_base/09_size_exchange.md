# Size Exchange Workflow & Non-Exchangeable Categories

Apparel and Footwear products are eligible for a one-time free size exchange within 7 days of delivery, subject to inventory availability in the Nykaa warehouse. If the requested replacement size is out of stock, the order is automatically converted to a standard refund workflow upon item return. Beauty products, skincare, intimate wear, and earrings are strictly non-exchangeable due to hygiene and health guidelines. The exchange shipment is dispatched as soon as the returned item passes quality verification at the logistics hub.
